# Renter flow (draft, for a later renter page)

Removed from the homepage on site-v3 (wording cut, Oct 22, 2026). Not live; HouseTour AI is in development.
Section heading used before: "Tour when it works for you." Lead: "How it will work, step by step. HouseTour AI is in development."
Side visual: Kira, "Ask anything" chips: Rent / Utilities / The neighbourhood (now its own homepage block).

1. **Pick a listing**: See the rent, photos and details.
2. **Tap 'Book a viewing'**
3. **Verify your ID**: Photo ID + selfie.
4. **Tell the landlord about you**: A short intro (what you do for work), move-in date, how many people are moving in, pets, smoking.
5. **Your credit score range** (optional): choices Under 600 / 600-679 / 680-749 / 750+ / Prefer not to say.
   Note: The full report comes later: once the landlord wants to move forward, you share your own report from Equifax, TransUnion or another bureau. HouseTour AI never runs credit checks.
6. **Pick a time**: Within the landlord's hours, then wait for approval.
7. **Tour in person**: With Kira answering your questions.

Copy locks: credit is always optional and renter-supplied; HouseTour AI never runs credit checks; never 'anytime'/'24/7' for tours; say 'In development'.
