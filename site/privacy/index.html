Privacy

Your privacy on the waitlist.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

HouseTour AI is in development. This notice explains, in plain English, what information we collect when you join the waitlist on this site and what we do with it.

Who we are

HouseTour AI is operated by Charter Residential, based in Toronto, Ontario. In this notice, “we”, “us” and “our” mean HouseTour AI and Charter Residential.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect:

  • your email address;
  • whether you chose renter or landlord / operator;
  • the signup source (which form on the site you used and, if you arrived through one of our links, which post or link it was) and the time you signed up.

Why we collect it

We use this information to keep your place on the waitlist and to send you email updates about HouseTour AI, such as early access in Toronto & GTA. Your renter or landlord / operator choice helps us send you the updates that are relevant to you.

We only email you because you asked to join. By submitting the form you give us your consent to receive these emails, in line with Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). You can withdraw that consent at any time.

Who stores it for us

Our waitlist is stored and our emails are sent using Kit (formerly ConvertKit), an email service provider. Kit handles this information on our behalf to run the waitlist and send our emails.

How long we keep it, and unsubscribing

We keep your information while you are on the waitlist. You can leave at any time: every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, or you can email us at ammo@aihousetour.com and we will remove you.

We don’t sell your data

We do not sell your personal information.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Just email ammo@aihousetour.com from the address you signed up with.

Questions

If you have any questions about this notice or how we handle your information, email ammo@aihousetour.com, or call or text us at +1 437 974 2863.

We may update this notice as HouseTour AI develops. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
